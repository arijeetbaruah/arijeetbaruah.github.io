    <div class="about-text reveal">
        <p>
        I'm a <strong>Gameplay and Tools Programmer</strong> from Guwahati, Assam.
        It started when I was 8 years old — I saw Mortal Kombat 2 and asked a question
        I couldn't let go of: <em>who makes this, and is it their job?</em> Turns out
        the answer is yes, and I've been working toward that ever since.
        </p>
        <p>
        My career spans commercial game studios and web product companies. On the games
        side I've shipped titles with <strong>Tentworks Interactive</strong> (Becoming
        Pablo), and
        <strong>Hungama Game Studio</strong> (Vineyard Valley, DesiBeats, Song Beat) — across mobile,
        PC, and Steam. Before that I worked at <strong>Webonise Labs</strong> building
        web platforms, including a music analytics tool for Sony Music Entertainment.
        </p>
        <p>
        What I care about most is the full stack of what makes a game <em>feel</em>
        right — the moment-to-moment sensation of playing, the interconnected systems
        that create emergent gameplay, and the internal tools that let designers and
        artists build without waiting on engineering. Clean architecture, reusable
        systems, and code that other developers can read and extend without a guided
        tour.
        </p>
        <p>
        I've worked in teams as small as 5 and as large as 300. Currently open to
        <strong>full-time and contract roles</strong> in gameplay programming, tools
        engineering, or game-adjacent engineering — remote-friendly.
        </p>
