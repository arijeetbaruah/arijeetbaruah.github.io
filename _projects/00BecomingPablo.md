Becoming Pablo is a turn-based, Grand-Strategy, upcoming Steam title where I worked as a gameplay programmer, focusing on building scalable systems and improving the overall player experience.<br>
<br>
I designed and implemented a Research & Development (tech tree) system to support progression and balancing, along with core gameplay systems such as Save/Load, Options Menu, and Loading Screens to ensure smooth state management and usability. I also integrated FMOD audio and Steam Achievements, connecting gameplay events to player feedback and platform features.<br>
<br>
Beyond gameplay systems, I contributed to overall performance and workflow by optimizing game initialization and loading flow, improving startup reliability and runtime stability. I also built a data-driven pipeline using Google Sheets to allow designers to manage and iterate on game data without code changes, supported by custom editor tools and validation systems for localization and content accuracy.<br><br>
