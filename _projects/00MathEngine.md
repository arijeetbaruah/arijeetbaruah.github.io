<div class="content">
    <p>
    Maths Engine is a modular system for building serializable mathematical formulas in Unity.<br/>
    Instead of hardcoding equations in scripts, you can create flexible formula graphs using reusable Math Nodes.<br/>
    This makes it ideal for systems like:
    </p>
    <ul>
        <li>Damage calculations</li>
        <li>AI decision formulas</li>
        <li>Gameplay balancing</li>
        <li>Procedural systems</li>
        <li>Conditional logic</li>
    </ul>
</div>
<div class="content">
<h2># 🚀 Features</h2>

<ul class="highlight-list">
    <li>Modular node-based math system</li>
    <li>Fully serializable formulas</li>
    <li>Runtime formula evaluation</li>
    <li>Human-readable equation generation</li>
    <li>Extensible custom math nodes</li>
    <li>Logical operators for conditional formulas</li>
    <li>Comparison operators</li>
</ul>
</div>
